See what needs attention

  • Click a label to see only its mail that is still in your inbox.
  • Labels with unread mail appear in bold at the top.
  • Choose conversation counts or individual-message counts.
  • Select and arrange labels, filter by a prefix, or hide empty labels.
  • Keep Gmail’s original label controls available.

Reading mail reduces the unread count. Archiving removes it from inbox totals after the next refresh.

Your email stays yours

The extension connects directly to Google to calculate counts. It does not request email bodies or modify your mail through the Gmail API. Preferences and cached counts are stored locally in Chrome. There is no developer-operated backend, advertising, or analytics.

Getting started

Once the Store release is available, install the extension, open its settings, and choose Connect Gmail. Then select your labels and open Gmail.

Designed for desktop Chrome and the English Gmail interface. Supports one connected Google account per Chrome profile. No Grok or other AI service is required.
