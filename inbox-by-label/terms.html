What the extension does

Inbox by label adds label-based views and unread and total inbox counts to Gmail. Clicking a label opens a search in Gmail. Counts may reflect a previous refresh and may be delayed, incomplete, or unavailable. Check Gmail directly when an accurate or current count matters.
